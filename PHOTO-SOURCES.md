The photo pages listed each image as free when the file was downloaded. The pictures show the finished bread, with no people. The naan photo includes a printed paper liner under the breads and no readable brand name. The garlic-knots photo is the original 1024×768 frame, compressed to WebP. No Pexels photo of garlic knots was available. The page links the file and the [CC BY 2.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) and says the picture was compressed. The sourdough-bagels photo is a plain bagel, resized to 1200×938. The New York pizza photo is one round pie, 1200×800. The calzone photo is a half-moon, 1200×800. The kaiser-rolls photo is one round roll with the five-part star on top, resized to 1200×1089 and converted to WebP. No Pexels photo of a kaiser roll was available. The page links the file and the [CC BY-SA 2.5 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/). The WebP is shared under that same license. The breadsticks photo is two long thin sticks, 1200×1800. The Roman pan pizza photo is cropped to the rectangular pizza, 1200×970. The lavash photo is cropped to the finished thin sheets, 1200×832. The original also shows a tandoor and part of a person; those are not in the file used here. The rye sourdough photo is dark loaves with a dense brown crumb, 1200×960, and it is not the seeded rye loaf on the yeasted rye page.
